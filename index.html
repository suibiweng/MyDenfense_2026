This was Suibi's doctoral dissertation defense presentation titled "Generating Your Reality: Advancing the Design of AI-Generated User Experience in Mixed Reality." Suibi presented three main projects that demonstrated how generative AI can expand beyond creating static assets in mixed reality toward interactive collaboration, behavior authoring, and perceptual transformation. The first project, Editing Reality, focused on real-time editing of the physical world through AI and multimodal interaction, with eight core capabilities: generate, iterate, reconstruct, retexture, sketch-based modeling, erase, anchor, and collaboration. The second project, DreamPortal, explored how generative AI can support behavior authoring in mixed reality by generating not only 3D objects but also runtime behavior scripts that define how objects interact with users. The third project, Hypervision, introduced the concept of generative re-rendering by processing users' pass-through views through diffusion models and regenerating them for the headset. During the question-and-answer session, committee members discussed technical challenges including visual quality issues, interaction modalities, and the future potential of these systems, particularly regarding the balance between AI capabilities and human creativity in mixed reality applications.
